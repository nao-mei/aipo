# AI PO Coach

A private, offline, mobile-first study app for **AI product ownership**: how a Product Owner owns a product that uses AI, not just how to use AI tools.

Live site: https://nao-mei.github.io/aipo/

## What it is

- Nine domains, from "what is an AI Product Owner?" through problem framing, evaluation, human-AI experience, risk, and release and operations.
- Lessons, a glossary, drills, scenarios, flashcards, spaced review, a study plan, a check-up, a self-scored capstone, and a readiness dashboard.
- Plain JavaScript. No frameworks, no accounts, no analytics, no network calls.
- Progress is stored only in the browser on your own device. Settings has a backup export and import.

## Important notes

- **All study content is original, AI-drafted and has NOT been human-reviewed.** Each item has been checked by automated review passes against the sources below, but a person should validate it before relying on it.
- It is **not official guidance** from any standards body, certification body or vendor, and it is **not legal, financial or regulatory advice**. Regulation is covered at awareness level only; for real decisions involve your own legal, compliance and privacy teams.
- The definition of the "AI Product Owner" role is a synthesis of the sources below. None of them uses that term.
- The app has not been tested on a real phone. Install and offline behaviour are expected to work but are unverified.

## Sources (concepts paraphrased, versions pinned)

- Scrum Guide, November 2020 (Product Owner accountabilities).
- NIST AI Risk Management Framework 1.0 (AI 100-1), January 2023. NIST has said a revision is under way; the Playbook will follow it.
- NIST AI 600-1, Generative AI Profile, July 2024.
- Google People + AI Guidebook (concepts paraphrased).
- Microsoft Research, Guidelines for Human-AI Interaction (only the count of 18 and the four phases are used).
- Scrum.org Evidence-Based Management Guide (concept names only; not read from the original).

## Use on a phone

Open the live site in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Study from the home-screen icon. Progress is tied to the web address, so it resets if the address changes.

See `HANDOVER.md` for how this site is maintained.
